Date: 2026-08-07   Commit: 11b775b

TWO METHOD NOTES, both earned during this sweep:

1. The client-boundary rules below were first written as RUNTIME assertions
   and failed: reading ScriptInstance.Source needs PluginOrOpenCloud
   capability, which a game script does not have. They were not wrong, they
   were in the wrong place. Anything provable only from the TEXT belongs in
   P-3, which greps from disk; anything provable from the loaded module
   belongs in UT-13.

2. EVERY PROHIBITION GREP MUST EXCLUDE COMMENTS. The cast check below first
   reported a violation, and the match was the source comment EXPLAINING why
   a cast must not be used. A grep that trips on the documentation of its own
   rule punishes writing the rule down. All text checks now strip comment
   lines before matching.
