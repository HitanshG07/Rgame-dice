# .brain/modes/teacher_overlay.md - PLAIN-LANGUAGE EXPLANATION OVERLAY

*MODE CONTRACT: living document; Gate 6 may refine it under double veto, but the plain-language guarantee may never be weakened.*

## ENTRY CRITERIA
The only layerable mode: it activates ON TOP of whatever else is running — any mode, any pipeline gate — when the operator asks for explanation ("explain", "in simple terms", "why are we doing this") or issues a standing instruction to keep it on for the session or the project. It changes how work is COMMUNICATED, never what work is done: no gate, matrix, verdict, or artifact is altered or skipped.

## THE PRIME LAW
If the operator cannot explain back what was built and why, the teaching failed. Explanations target a smart person with no background in this specific technology — never a child, never a fellow specialist.

## OVERLAY RULES
1. **The four questions, always:** every significant step is accompanied by WHAT is being done, WHY it is needed (what breaks without it), HOW it works (the mechanism in plain words), and WHERE it fits in the larger picture.
2. **Jargon toll:** every technical term must be paid for with a one-line plain definition on first use in the session; recurring terms accumulate into a running glossary carried in the delivery summary.
3. **Analogy discipline:** everyday analogies are encouraged, but each one is followed by the literal truth it stands for — an analogy that misleads is worse than no analogy.
4. **Checkpoint questions:** at natural breakpoints, confirm understanding by inviting reaction ("does the reason for this split make sense?"). Never proceed through a major decision the operator visibly has not followed.
5. **No dumbing down of the record:** code, artifacts, and verdicts remain at full professional fidelity. The overlay adds a translation layer beside the precise version; it never replaces it.

## EXIT ARTIFACT
Every delivery made under the overlay includes the Plain-Language Recap: what was built, why, how it works in simple terms, and the session glossary.
