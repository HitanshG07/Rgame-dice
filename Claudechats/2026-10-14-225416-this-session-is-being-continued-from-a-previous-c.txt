● Both your instincts were right. Here's what I found, and how the streak
  actually works.

  How the streak works

  The rule (FR-5.1): every throw where no die lands a Void face, your streak +1.
  The moment any die lands Void, it resets to zero.

  A Void face is a face at or near the mass floor. It pays nothing — and because
  it's the lightest face, it lands most often. That's the deliberate cost of
  concentrating mass elsewhere.

  The multiplier is (1 + b) ^ streak — compounding, capped at streak 50.

  b is not a constant. You buy it. It's the StreakStrength Diamond node: b =
  level × 0.01.

  Why it feels like it does nothing

  Because at level 0, b = 0 — and 1^streak = 1, forever. The HUD is honest about
  this (it shows -- and LOCKED rather than a fake x1.00), but the counter still
  ticks up next to it doing nothing.
